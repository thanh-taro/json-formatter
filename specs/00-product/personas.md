# Personas

- **PER-01** — *Working developer.* Pastes API responses, log lines and config files several
  times a day, often containing tokens or personal data and sometimes tens of megabytes. Wants
  a readable view or the exact location of a syntax error in seconds; distrusts sites that
  upload data; notices when 64-bit IDs get rounded.
- **PER-02** — *Portal maintainer.* Owns the developer-tools portal that embeds this tool. Wants
  it to look native in the portal (theme, sizing), need no backend, and stay out of the way.
